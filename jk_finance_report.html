Observations and recommendations

Growth, margin expansion and lower finance costs all moved the right way, and leverage is low. Working capital is the main drag: about ₹1,400 lakhs is tied up in inventory and receivables.

  1. Cut receivable days from 59 to about 50 to release roughly ₹120 lakhs.
  2. Reduce inventory days from 78 to about 65 with better demand planning.
  3. Prepay part of the long-term loan if surplus cash builds.
  4. Tie capex to returns, with a payback period under four years.
  5. Report monthly and keep a rolling 12-month cash forecast.
